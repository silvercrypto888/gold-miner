% ─── Abstract ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section*{Abstract}

Gold Miner is an on-chain multiplayer fair mine game deployed on the X1 Network (Solana VM). Players navigate a 1,024 $\times$ 1,024 grid, discovering and mining gold spots that yield GOLD tokens (Token-2022). Gold spots are consumable --- once mined, they never respawn for that world epoch. This creates a spatial competition where players must balance exploration, path optimization, and speed against other participants. All minted GOLD that is routed through the treasury goes to protocol-owned liquidity and permanently burned, creating a deflationary token economy.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Key properties:} permissionless entry, deterministic world generation, session-key gameplay, auto-LP burn treasury, and permissionless bitmap reset at 75\% exhaustion.

% ─── 1. Introduction ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Introduction}

Most blockchain games fall into two categories: fully on-chain but mechanically simple (turn-based, deterministic), or visually rich but mostly off-chain ( centralized state, token-gated ). Gold Miner pursues a third path: a real-time spatial competition game where every move is recorded on-chain, yet the player experience is fluid and responsive.

The game is played on a 1,024 $\times$ 1,024 grid. Each cell is either empty or contains gold, determined by a public formula: $(x \ \&\ \ y) \ \% \ 7 = 0$. This deterministic generation means every player sees the same world, and foresight --- knowing where gold lies ahead --- becomes a strategic tool.

Players begin at position $(1,1)$. From there, they move using arrow keys or WASD. Each move is an on-chain transaction signed by an ephemeral session key, eliminating the need for a wallet popup on every action. When a player steps on a gold spot, the contract automatically mints 100 GOLD tokens (Token-2022) to their wallet.

% ─── 2. Core Mechanics ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Core Mechanics}

\subsection{The Grid}

The game world is a discrete grid with the following properties:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Property & Value \\
\midrule
Dimensions & 1,024 $\times$ 1,024 cells \\
Total cells & 1,048,576 \\
Gold formula & $(x \ \&\ \ y) \ \% \ 7 = 0$ \\
Gold per spot & 100 GOLD \\
Total supply cap & $\sim$150 million GOLD (at 100\% grid exhaustion) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The bitwise-AND modulo formula produces a scattered but deterministic pattern. Players can compute gold locations locally, enabling strategic path planning without revealing information to others.

\subsection{Consumable Resources}

Gold spots are consumed on first mine. Once a cell has been mined, it yields no further GOLD for the remainder of the world epoch. This is the central competitive mechanism.

The consequence is immediate: multiple players converging on the same region compete for a finite resource. Early arrival is rewarded not by a multiplier but by \textit{access to unmined territory}. Late arrivals find only empty cells.

\subsection{Spatial Diffusion Incentive}

The Nash equilibrium of the game is for players to spread out across the map. Clustering is suboptimal because:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Shared finite spots lower per-player yield
\item Travel time to unmined areas increases with local density
\item First-mover advantage in any region belongs to whoever arrived first
\end{itemize}

This mirrors real-world resource competition --- gold rushes, mining claims, territorial exploration --- where knowledge and speed compound.

\subsection{Session Keys}

To avoid requiring a wallet signature on every move, Gold Miner uses ephemeral session keys:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Player connects wallet and signs one transaction to join
\item Contract generates an encrypted session keypair (AES-256-GCM via Web Crypto API)
\item Session key signs all subsequent move transactions
\item Session expires after $\sim$4 hours (36,000 slots)
\item Player can start a new session at any time with one wallet signature
\end{enumerate}

This preserves non-custodial security while enabling fluid gameplay.

% ─── 3. Token Economics ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Token Economics}

\subsection{GOLD Token}

GOLD is a Token-2022 (SPL Token-2022 program) mint with the following parameters:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Parameter & Value \\
\midrule
Name & Goldium \\
Symbol & GOLD \\
Decimals & 9 \\
Mint authority & Game program (mint-on-demand) \\
Freeze authority & None \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Tokens are minted exclusively through gameplay. There is no pre-mine, no airdrop, no founder allocation. The only source of new GOLD is mining gold spots on the grid.

\subsection{Protocol Treasury and Auto-LP Burn}

All mined GOLD flows through the following pipeline:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Player mines gold $\rightarrow$ 100 GOLD minted to their wallet
\item Simulatneously, 100 GOLD minted to the protocol treasury
\item Treasury automatically swaps GOLD for XNT via AMM
\item LP tokens are deposited into protocol-owned liquidity
\item LP tokens are permanently burned
\end{enumerate}

This creates a deflationary flywheel: more gameplay $\rightarrow$ more treasury volume $\rightarrow$ deeper liquidity $\rightarrow$ lower slippage $\rightarrow$ better player experience.

\subsection{Bitmap Reset}

When 75\% of gold spots have been mined, any player can call \texttt{reset\_mineable\_area()} to:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item Zero the on-chain bitmap tracking mined cells
\item Reset the exhaustion counter
\item Allow gold to respawn across the grid
\end{itemize}

This is permissionless and trustless. No admin key, no governance vote. The game self-resets when the world is sufficiently exhausted, preserving long-term playability.

\section{Architecture}

\subsection{Smart Contract}

The game is built with Anchor on Solana VM and deployed on X1. Key program instructions:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{8cm}@{}}
\toprule
Instruction & Description \\
\midrule
\texttt{initialize\_game} & One-time setup: grid size, gold formula parameters, token config \\
\texttt{join\_game} & Player onboarding: creates player state, initializes session key \\
\texttt{move\_and\_mine} & Core gameplay: validates move, checks gold spot, mints if eligible \\
\texttt{deposit\_xnt} & Funds player gas account for movement fees \\
\texttt{withdraw\_xnt} & Returns unused gas to player wallet \\
\texttt{reset\_mineable\_area} & Permissionless bitmap reset at 75\% exhaustion \\
\texttt{treasury\_auto\_lp} & Converts treasury GOLD to protocol-owned LP + burn \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The client is a Next.js application with TypeScript and TailwindCSS. It handles:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]

\item Session key generation and encryption
\item Grid rendering with WebGL-accelerated canvas
\item Move batching and RPC optimization
\item Real-time position and balance updates
\end{itemize}

% ─── 5. Security Model ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Security Model}

\subsection{Player Protections}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Non-custodial:} Player retains full control of wallet and funds at all times
\item \textbf{Session key expiry:} Ephemeral keys automatically expire, limiting scope of compromise
\item \textbf{Encrypted storage:} Session keys are AES-256-GCM encrypted in browser localStorage
\item \textbf{No admin drain:} No privileged instruction can transfer player funds
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Game Integrity}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Deterministic world:} Gold locations are computed on-chain from public formula --- no oracle, no randomness, no manipulation
\item \textbf{Consumable verification:} Bitmap account is updated atomically with mint --- double-mining is impossible
\item \textbf{Permissionless reset:} No single party controls world state transitions
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Economic Safeguards}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Mint-on-demand only:} No pre-mine, no hidden inflation
\item \textbf{Protocol-owned liquidity:} Treasury funds are LP'd and burned, not held
\item \textbf{Immutable config option:} Game parameters can be locked via \texttt{finalize\_game()} to prevent post-launch changes
\end{itemize}

% ─── 8. Conclusion ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Conclusion}

Gold Miner demonstrates that on-chain games need not sacrifice mechanical depth for trustlessness. By treating the blockchain as a deterministic, shared game server rather than a settlement layer, we achieve:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item True ownership of mined assets
\item Verifiable scarcity and fair distribution
\item Censorship-resistant gameplay
\item Protocol-aligned incentives through auto-LP burn
\end{itemize}

The consumable-resource model ensures competition emerges endogenously from the rules rather than from artificial leaderboards or time-limited events. Every player starts at the same position with the same information. What separates them is speed, strategy, and execution.
